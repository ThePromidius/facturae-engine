Legal Registry: Spanish Invoicing Laws
======================================

This registry contains the raw legal requirements as published in the Official State Gazette (BOE) and updated technical regulations.

.. note::
   **Last Review Date:** 2026-05-13
   **Current Status:** Up-to-date with Real Decreto-ley 15/2025.

Active Regulations
------------------

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

*   **Core Purpose:** Defines the "SIF" (Sistemas Informáticos de Facturación) and the "VERI*FACTU" voluntary submission system.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
*   **Status:** Active with Corrigendum (2024-11-08)

*   **Reference:** :doc:`context/BOE-A-2024-22138` and :doc:`context/BOE-A-2024-23180`

Critical Deadlines (2025/2026 Update)
-------------------------------------

As per **Real Decreto-ley 15/2025**, the mandatory implementation dates have been shifted:

*   **Software Producers:** Must offer compliant software by **mid-2026** (9 months after technical order entry).
*   **Large Taxpayers (Art 3.1.a):** Mandatory by **January 1, 2027**.
*   **SMEs and Freelancers:** Mandatory by **July 1, 2027**.

Technical Integrity Articles
----------------------------

.. _hac_1177_art7:

Art. 7: Traceability (Chaining)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Each record must link to the previous one using:
1. NIF of the issuer.
2. Number and series of the previous invoice.
3. Date of the previous invoice.
4. First 64 characters of the previous record's hash.

.. _hac_1177_art13:

Art. 13: Fingerprint (Huella) Formula
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The hash is SHA-256. The concatenation string MUST follow the order specified in the FAQ and technical manual.

.. _hac_1177_art14:

Art. 14: Electronic Signature
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
*   **Standard:** ETSI EN 319 132.
*   **Type:** XAdES Enveloped Signature.
*   **Certificate:** Must be a qualified certificate in the EU Trusted List.
